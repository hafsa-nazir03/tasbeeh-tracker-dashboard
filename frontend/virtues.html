Importance of Zikar

Discover the timeless guidance of the Qur'an and authentic Hadith that highlight the beauty, rewards, and importance of remembering Allah.

📖Qur'an Verses

1. Allah remembers those who remember Him.

❝ Remember Me; I will remember you. ❞

📍 Surah Al-Baqarah (2:152)

2. Remember Allah often.

❝ And remember Allah often so that you may succeed. ❞

📍 Surah Al-Anfal (8:45)

3. The remembrance of Allah is greatest.

❝ Indeed, prayer prevents immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater. ❞

📍 Surah Al-'Ankabut (29:45)

🤲Authentic Ahadith

1. The living and the dead

❝ The example of the one who remembers his Lord and the one who does not is like the living and the dead. ❞

📚 Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6407; Sahih Muslim, Hadith 779

2. Keep your tongue moist with Dhikr

❝ Keep your tongue moist with the remembrance of Allah. ❞

📚 Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3375 (graded Hasan)
